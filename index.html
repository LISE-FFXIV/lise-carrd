LISE
EURELT
Veil of Secrets

How beautiful the nobility is, adorned in its veil of secrets.

“Guerrique, do you know why they named me

the Veil of Secrets?”

“Snow buries sins beneath its white shroud.”

To some, Lise is a servant sold to the Holy See; to others, she is one who walks the edge of heresy. Both claims hold some truth: the peace of Ishgard is her very reason for living. Every day, she risks her life, fighting in the hope of bringing better days to her people. Yet she rejects the weight of old traditions, using her status in the Church to move in the shadows; she pretends to obey and pray, before surrendering to secret vices, far from prying eyes.

An Inquisitor by title, she is a spy, shadowy guardian of shameful secrets, averting trouble before it arises, whether internal or external to Ishgard—by leading a group of spies with a velvet-gloved iron fist. However, it is not uncommon for her to secretly take liberties, exploiting her high-ranking position to silence those who would try to muzzle her. What the Holy See condemns as heresy may not trouble Lise at all; she allows many vices to slip through her net if they don’t disrupt the peace she so desperately seeks.

“I bury the vices of men and the truths they are too afraid to face.”

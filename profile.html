Lise is a fucking brat, plain and simple. Whilst she’s a fiercely loyal friend, she’s nonetheless a particularly unpredictable and exasperating free spirit. She lives by her own rules: does what she wants, when she wants, how she wants and the most infuriating part? She’s good at it, leaving little room to argue with the results. She obeys - partially, hunts, kills, lies, and bends the truth to fit her own. She drinks, smokes, indulges in heretical pleasures, and shows herself to be curious and open to change despite her firmly held beliefs.

She’s a strong woman who masks her insecurities and lack of confidence behind an overwhelming extraversion; sometimes quiet, sometimes loud once she feels comfortable. One time she’s the one you have to carry home on your back; the next, she’s the one who’ll let you spend the night on her sofa. A woman of many faces and identities; a troublesome brat eager to sink her teeth into life, full of contradictions and paradoxes, capable of unwavering professionalism as much as unrestrained chaos when she lets herself slip.

Loyal ally or merciless huntress - depends entirely on the angle you choose.
